The Judge

Fade the trade, or not?

What Catch22 is doing today

Why does fading pay off?

C22 uses its participants as market signals: when a trader buys gold at $1,900 as a losing position, C22 fades it.

500
Clients

Day-long “campaign”
trading FICC (e.g. Gold)

-4%Loss Limit

Hard drawdown ceiling

trades via

C22 Trading Platform

(the platform C22 controls)

Markov Chain Judge
Hard-coded rules +
Founder’s judgement
Decides to either

FADE

House holds the loser internally, captures the client’s loss, pays no spread

PASS

Trade expected to win, or unclear

Problem Space

Catch22’s entire alpha depends on one critical decision:

“Which client’s trade do we fade (reverse) to maximise profit?”

Pain points

The existing system is heavily constrained

1

Static rules: a system that can’t learn

Hard-coded rules go stale; they can’t evolve with new trading patterns across campaigns.

2

Current models have plateaued

Catch22 needs a fresh perspective to keep improving.

Methodological Guardrails

How we keep results honest

Blocked time-series CV

Never split a campaign or trader across train/test sets

Point-in-time features only

Strictly no look-ahead leakage in the feature set

Confidence intervals, not win rates
